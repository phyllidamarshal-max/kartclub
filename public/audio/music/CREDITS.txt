KART CLUB - Living scene soundtracks (revision 8)

Revision 8 rebuilds all nineteen map scores with 380 newly written musical bars
and independent environmental stems. Instrumental and environmental layers
start, loop and crossfade together. Previous editions are described below for
historical rebuilds; their recordings are retained only in the comparison folder.

Environmental recording credits:
- Ambient Bird Sounds by isaiah658, CC0 1.0.
  https://opengameart.org/content/ambient-bird-sounds
- Birds forest by Barracuda1983, public-domain dedication by its author.
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Birds_forest.ogg
- Beach Ocean Waves by jasinski; excerpt submitted by qubodup, CC0 1.0.
  https://opengameart.org/content/beach-ocean-waves
- Icy Heights: wind.ogg ONLY by Ecrivain, CC0 1.0.
  https://opengameart.org/content/icy-heights
  The third-party musical theme from this page is NOT included.
CC0 license: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

These recordings are cropped, filtered, faded and spatially arranged. Additional
gulls, small creature calls, leaf/grass/snow/sand textures, magma bubbles, ice
resonance, machinery, rail sounds, crowd murmur and station effects are original
designed/synthesized sound effects, not field recordings. No intelligible crowd
dialogue is included. Source file checksums: environment-sources.json.

Current rebuild: scripts/music/render_living.py
Checks: scripts/music/check_living.py and scripts/music/validate_living.py
Local installation: scripts/music/publish_living.py
Paired and isolated previews: output/music/living-v8/

Historical editions:

Revision 7 adds ten circuit-specific compositions with 240 newly authored bars.
Each has independent A, B and bridge melodies, chord progressions, bass parts
and percussion. The existing nine revision-6 audio masters are retained.
New scene arrangements include orchard bluegrass, tropical causeway samba,
street electro swing, canopy marimba breaks, canyon guitar, crystalline liquid
breaks, a nautical fanfare, clockwork electro, chiptune and a train shuffle.
These are original stylized game scores rendered with the licensed instruments
below. No additional third-party recordings are included.
Rebuild new tracks: scripts/music/render_expansion.py
Validate: scripts/music/check_expansion_composition.py and
scripts/music/validate.py --expansion
Install locally: scripts/music/publish_expansion.py
New-score previews: output/music/expansion-v7/*-melody.wav and *-highlight.wav
The revision-6 commands below are retained for rebuilding the original edition.


Revision 6 replaces all nine scene compositions with 216 authored bars across
independent A, B and bridge sections. Loops use different forms and meters.
The desert piece uses Phrygian-dominant color, plucked strings, a reed patch and
hand drums. It is an original cinematic scene score, not an authentic maqam
performance or a recording of traditional regional instruments.
Rebuild: scripts/music/render_worlds.py; validate: scripts/music/validate.py --staging;
install locally: scripts/music/publish_worlds.py. New melodies can be compared on
one piano, key and tempo in output/music/world-v6/*-melody.wav.

Lobby addition: Clubhouse Sunshine (revision 1), original 132 BPM C-major piano/guitar theme.
The lobby cue uses the same licensed FluidR3 GM instruments listed below.
Interface tap, select, confirm and back cues are original synthesized sounds.

Original melodies and arrangements created for KART CLUB. No KartRider audio recordings or melodies are included.

Instrument samples: FluidR3 GM by Frank Wen and contributors, MIT license.
